# Taiwan Wellness And Beauty Compliance Notes

This is a risk-screening aid, not legal advice or an approval list. Example phrases below are draft candidates only; they are not automatically lawful or suitable for a particular product, evidence set, audience, or channel. For public-facing advertising, labeling, e-commerce, livestream, or distributor material, verify the current requirements with the relevant official Taiwan sources (including MOHW/TFDA where applicable) and preserve the source and review date. Ask the user for approved wording when available.

Do not convert a risky claim into a suggested phrase and silently treat the result as approved. Keep the original, proposed alternative, supporting evidence, and review status distinguishable. If current requirements or product-specific facts cannot be verified, mark the wording for human review or omit it from public-facing copy.

## Risk Lens

Flag and soften wording when content implies:

- Disease prevention, diagnosis, treatment, cure, or symptom relief.
- Effects on diabetes, hypertension, cancer, liver/kidney disease, immune disease, depression, insomnia, or other medical conditions.
- Guaranteed results, rapid transformation, permanent effects, or universal outcomes.
- Drug-like mechanisms or clinical endpoints without approved evidence.
- Before/after claims that imply medical or dramatic body changes.
- Cosmetic claims that cross into tissue repair, inflammation treatment, medication-like whitening, hair regrowth, hormone regulation, or wound healing.

## Health Food / Supplement Wording

The examples in this section are prompts for cautious drafting, not a safe-harbor list. Check the product's category, approved status, evidence, and intended communication channel before using any phrase.

Prefer:

- "支持日常營養補給"
- "幫助維持健康狀態"
- "調整體質"
- "促進新陳代謝"
- "幫助消化道機能"
- "作為日常保養的一環"
- "依照建議用量持續補充"
- "搭配均衡飲食與規律作息"

Avoid or verify carefully:

- "治療", "改善疾病", "預防疾病", "降血糖", "降血壓", "抗癌"
- "消炎", "止痛", "修復器官", "排毒治病"
- "保證有效", "立即見效", "完全根治"
- Overstated immune wording such as "提升免疫力不生病"

Safer transformations:

- "降低血糖" -> "調節生理機能，支持健康代謝"
- "治療便秘" -> "幫助消化道機能，維持順暢感"
- "抗發炎" -> "支持日常防護與健康平衡"
- "減肥" -> "輔助體態管理，搭配飲食與運動"

## Cosmetic / Skincare Wording

The examples in this section are prompts for cautious drafting, not a safe-harbor list. Check the product's category, substantiation, and intended communication channel before using any phrase.

Prefer:

- "保濕", "滋潤", "舒緩肌膚乾燥不適"
- "使肌膚柔嫩、明亮、有光澤"
- "修護肌膚屏障" only when used as cosmetic barrier-care language and not wound/medical repair.
- "改善乾燥造成的細紋視覺"
- "維持肌膚油水平衡"

Avoid or verify carefully:

- "治療濕疹/痘痘/皮膚炎"
- "消炎", "殺菌治痘", "修復傷口"
- "永久美白", "抑制黑色素生成" if framed as drug-like effect.
- "再生", "逆齡", "回春" when presented as biological certainty.

Safer transformations:

- "治療痘痘" -> "幫助調理油水平衡，減少悶黏感"
- "消炎修復" -> "舒緩乾燥不適，維持肌膚屏障"
- "美白淡斑" -> "提亮膚色視覺，展現明亮光澤"
- "抗老逆齡" -> "撫平乾燥細紋視覺，提升彈潤感"

## Evidence Language

Use evidence carefully:

- Say "研究指出某成分具有..." only when a cited study exists and the wording matches the study.
- Separate ingredient evidence from finished-product evidence.
- Do not imply human clinical efficacy if evidence is in vitro, animal, supplier white paper, or unrelated dosage.
- For decks, label evidence level: "文獻機制", "原料資料", "人體試驗", "產品檢測".
- Keep a source record for each material claim, figure, comparison, and certification. Distinguish evidence about an ingredient from evidence about the finished product, and show a concise citation or source note on the slide where it is used.
- If a source, date, population, dose, or comparison basis is missing, do not fill it from memory. Mark it as unverified in working notes or leave the claim out of public-facing copy.

## Required Tone

For Taiwan business decks, sound credible rather than evasive:

- Replace medical certainty with "支持", "幫助", "維持", "調節", "日常保養".
- Keep disclaimers short and visible on claim-sensitive slides.
- Use "非醫療建議" or "產品非藥品" only when appropriate; do not use disclaimers to justify illegal claims.

## When To Browse

Browse official sources when:

- The user asks whether a specific claim is legal.
- The final deck will be used as public advertising, labeling, e-commerce, livestream, or distributor copy.
- You are unsure whether a phrase is currently allowed in Taiwan.
- The product claims to be officially certified, registered, or approved.
