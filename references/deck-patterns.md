# Deck Patterns

Use these patterns to convert Markdown content and DM visual language into an actual presentation.

If the Markdown already provides explicit slide headings or sections, use them as the source of truth for deck content. Choose a pattern to improve flow and visual treatment, not to add process or analysis pages.

## Pattern A: Sales Briefing

Best for distributor, clinic, counter, sales team, or buyer presentation.

1. Cover: product hero, DM-derived motif, one clear promise.
2. Market tension: audience pain point or category trend.
3. Consumer insight: why people need this product now.
4. Formula logic: key ingredients and roles.
5. Mechanism / beauty logic: one simplified diagram.
6. Proof: evidence, tests, certifications, or source credibility.
7. Product superiority: comparison with common alternatives.
8. Usage / SKU: how to use, who it fits, routine placement.
9. Sales talking points: compliant benefit bullets.
10. Closing CTA: order, consultation, campaign, or next step.

## Pattern B: Product Launch

Best for brand events, campaign decks, and partner launch briefings.

1. Brand mood cover.
2. Launch statement.
3. Problem and cultural context.
4. Product reveal.
5. Design/formula inspiration.
6. Ingredient or technology story.
7. Key benefits.
8. Hero visuals / KV adaptation.
9. Channel assets or campaign rollout.
10. CTA.

## Pattern C: Scientific Commercial

Best when the Markdown contains mechanisms, literature, formulation rationale, or clinical material.

1. Core question.
2. Biology/skin/nutrition mechanism.
3. Ingredient map.
4. Evidence hierarchy.
5. Product formula rationale.
6. User scenario.
7. Claim-safe commercial message.
8. FAQ / objections.
9. Summary.

## Pattern D: Training Deck

Best for internal staff, distributor education, customer service, or livestream hosts.

1. Product positioning.
2. Category basics.
3. Ingredient education.
4. How to explain benefits safely.
5. Use scenarios.
6. Customer Q&A.
7. Do-not-say claim list.
8. Demo or selling script.
9. Quiz/checklist.

## Pattern E: HTML Showcase

Best for shareable product story, live demo, or design-forward concept.

1. Full-viewport cover with product pack.
2. Product information or positioning section.
3. Benefit or formula story sections.
4. Mechanism diagram when supported by the Markdown.
5. Evidence or credibility wall when provided.
6. Routine/use or audience section.
7. Claim-safe talking points.
8. CTA or closing summary.

Keep HTML decks lightweight and product-first. Use DM-derived design tokens as CSS variables. Do not add a Visual DNA, creative brief, or extraction-method page unless the user asks to present design analysis.

## Layout Families

Use 4-8 repeatable layouts instead of inventing every slide:

- Product hero: packshot + large headline + 2-3 proof chips.
- Ingredient trio: 3 cards with ingredient, role, evidence cue.
- Mechanism ribbon: left narrative + right diagram.
- Evidence wall: study/test/certification cards with source labels.
- Routine steps: morning/night or before/during/after.
- Before/after: controlled visual comparison with visible caveats.
- FAQ grid: concern -> answer -> compliant phrase.
- Closing CTA: product, contact, QR, campaign message.

## Taiwan Chinese Deck Rhythm

- Use short slide titles: 8-16 Chinese characters when possible.
- Prefer "一頁一訊息".
- Turn long Markdown paragraphs into hierarchy: headline, subhead, 3 bullets.
- Use Traditional Chinese unless the user requests otherwise.
- Avoid overly abstract Western SaaS styling for consumer wellness products; keep product and benefit concrete.
